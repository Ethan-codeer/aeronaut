AERONAUT modifications, 2026-10-24:
Converted native AC3D exterior and gear; excluded hidden cabin/interior/effects.
Refined curvature with fixed boundaries; high LOD reduced with UV boundaries.
Merged shared materials by aircraft component, retained four engines and gear.
Created 2048x2048 and 512x512 texture atlases. Fuselage and wing artwork retain
upstream GPL licensing. Engine/gear/tyre artwork is original replacement artwork:
no original engine photographs, GE logo, airline paint or airline logo used.
Compressed geometry using locally bundled Meshopt; textures are embedded PNG.
Generated the 2D poster from the same converted exterior geometry and textures.

All modified model data, replacement texture artwork and the derivative poster
are distributed under GPL version 2. Corresponding editable model source,
texture-generation/conversion scripts and modification notices are provided at
/models/boeing747/corresponding-source.zip, alongside the full license.
The independent website/Three.js viewer remains separate from this asset work.

license is claimed. Boeing and 747 are used to identify the depicted aircraft.
This community model has simplifications; it is not CAD, engineering, maintenance
or certified flight-training material. No interior or gear animation is included.
