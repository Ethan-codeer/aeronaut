AERONAUT V1.3 — original teaching meshes

These seven assets are independently generated schematic exterior models, parameterized for the named subtype's public overall dimensions. They are NOT detailed manufacturer geometry. The meshes intentionally simplify nose sections, aerofoils, sweep transitions, nacelles, pylons, landing gear and cockpit glazing. They must be presented with the schematic limitation visible.

A320neo: sharklet-style upper tips, two engines, narrow-body dimensions.
A350-900: wide-body dimensions, curved raised tips, cockpit mask, four-wheel main bogies.
A380-800: full double-deck oval section, four engines, fence-style tips, five landing-gear units.
737 MAX 8: narrow body, split upper/lower tip devices, schematic nacelle chevrons, low stance.
787-9: wide-body dimensions, raked tips, schematic nacelle chevrons, larger cabin windows.
C919: narrow body dimensions, raised tips and cockpit mask.
E195-E2: narrow/fine body and large engines, long high-aspect wing, unwingleted raked tips.

The only available paint designs are original AERONAUT Ocean and AERONAUT Sunset. BOTH are Concept Livery designs; neither represents a real operator, a historical paint scheme, or manufacturer endorsement. Paint is supplied as actual mesh materials (AeronautPaint / AeronautAccent), not a 2D photo masquerading as a UV texture. Switching those colors requires no geometry reload. Upper hull white, lower hull navy, turquoise cheatline and fin flash, glazing, fan, gear and metal detail are independently batched.

Mesh parts have partId metadata; selecting highlights their schematic external mesh geometry. No animations, independent hinged surfaces, cabin interiors or engineering internals are included. Do not advertise gear/flap/rudder motion or engineering precision.

Physical length/span/height bounds use public dimensional values already linked to manufacturer references by Aircraft Atlas. Model and paint rights: CC0-1.0. Generator is included under MIT. No airline logo, third-party image texture or external model is used. Poster files are Blender offline renders of the same GLBs. The model and original paint schemes were authored for AERONAUT with OpenAI Codex assistance.

Resource measurements (not frame-rate certification): 17,512–47,512 triangles, 25–41 mesh-material draws, 0 image texture dependencies, each GLB below 901 KB. Real iPad A12X Safari performance and touch QA remain a separate required device test.
